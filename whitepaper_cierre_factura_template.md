## I. Executive Positioning (Client-Facing)

### Expanded Workstream Architecture and Firm-Sustained Institutional Support
This matter required substantially more than pleadings or isolated filings. It required continuous legal judgment, operational presence, documentary control, institutional pressure, and client-side stabilization across a file that moved from emergency detention response to patrimonial recovery, from customs resistance to constitutional escalation, and from prolonged administrative persistence to final material recovery.

The professional burden assumed by counsel was therefore not confined to visible outputs. It also included less visible but indispensable continuity work to keep the matter coherent, active, evidence-ready, and strategically viable while institutional resistance shifted across fronts.

For that reason, this closing record does not reduce the engagement to a retail-style list of gestures. It reflects a premium full-cycle intervention in which legal services, operational continuity, strategic supervision, and firm-sustained institutional support formed one recovery architecture.
